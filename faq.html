Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our fully funded distributed energy solutions.

A fully funded solution means Catalyst and our funding partners (Opele Energy and Jade-Sky) cover 100% of the capital cost of your distributed energy project. As a property owner, you pay nothing upfront — instead, you benefit from reduced energy costs through a long-term power purchase agreement (PPA) or lease structure.
No. Our solutions are designed to require zero capital outlay from property owners. All project costs are funded by our institutional capital partners, preserving your balance sheet capacity for core property investments and development.
We work with commercial, industrial, and institutional property portfolios including shopping centres, warehouses, logistics parks, office buildings, hospitals, and mixed-use developments. Generally, properties with large roof areas and significant electricity consumption are ideal candidates.
Catalyst acts as your single commercial partner — identifying opportunities, structuring deals, and managing the client relationship. Opele Energy and Jade-Sky provide funding, engineering, construction, and long-term operations. You deal with one partner (Catalyst) while benefiting from the full integrated capability of all three organisations.
From initial portfolio assessment to commissioning, a typical project takes 12–18 months. The origination and structuring phase takes 2–3 months, funding close takes 1–2 months, and construction and commissioning takes 6–12 months depending on project size.
We offer power purchase agreements (PPAs), energy lease structures, and off-balance sheet funding models. The optimal structure depends on your portfolio's financial requirements, tax position, and strategic objectives. Our commercial team will recommend the best approach for your situation.
Under our funded model, the system is owned and operated by our funding partners for the duration of the agreement. Opele Energy and Jade-Sky handle all operations, maintenance, and performance monitoring — ensuring optimal system performance throughout the project lifecycle.
