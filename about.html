Who We Are

Connecting Capital, Powering Infrastructure, Building Value.

Catalyst Infrastructure Partners is a commercial leader in distributed energy solutions across Southern Africa. We work with institutional property owners, REITs, and portfolio managers to identify, structure, and deliver fully funded renewable energy projects that transform energy from a cost burden into a strategic asset.

Our integrated partnership model brings together Catalyst's commercial expertise with Opele Energy and Jade-Sky's funding and delivery capabilities — creating a seamless end-to-end solution for institutional portfolios.

  • End-to-end commercial and technical capability
