Step 1

Originate

Catalyst's commercial team conducts a comprehensive assessment of your property portfolio to identify high-value distributed energy opportunities. We analyse roof space, load profiles, grid connectivity, and regulatory requirements across each asset.

  • Portfolio-wide energy audit
  • Site feasibility assessments
  • Load and consumption analysis
  • Regulatory and grid connection review
  • Prioritised opportunity ranking
Step 2

Structure

We design the optimal technical, commercial, and financial solution for each opportunity — tailoring the structure to your portfolio's specific requirements, financial constraints, and strategic objectives.

  • Technical system design
  • Commercial model selection (PPA, lease, etc.)
  • Financial modelling and investment case
  • Risk allocation and mitigation
  • Board-ready investment proposal
Step 3

Fund

Through our partnership with Opele Energy and Jade-Sky, we secure 100% funding for your distributed energy projects — with no capital outlay required from property owners. Your balance sheet remains free for core property investments.

  • Institutional capital deployment
  • Off-balance sheet structures
  • Zero upfront capital from property owner
  • Long-term PPA or lease agreements
  • Risk-sharing with funding partners
Step 4

Deliver

Opele Energy and Jade-Sky execute the full engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning, and long-term operations of your distributed energy assets — ensuring optimal performance over the project lifecycle.

  • Detailed engineering design
  • EPC / EPCM project delivery
  • Commissioning and grid connection
  • Performance monitoring
  • Long-term O&M and optimisation
